Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about my services

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the project's complexity. A standard 5-page website typically takes 2 to 3 weeks, while a custom web application or e-commerce platform may take 4 to 8 weeks to complete.

Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes, I offer ongoing maintenance and support packages to ensure your website remains secure, up-to-date, and optimized for performance long after the initial launch.

What is your payment structure?

I usually request a 50% upfront deposit to secure the project and begin work. The remaining 50% is due upon project completion and your final approval, right before deploying to the live server.

Can you improve my existing website's SEO?

Absolutely. I provide comprehensive SEO audits and optimizations, including on-page setup, speed optimization, and schema markup integration to improve your organic search engine rankings.
